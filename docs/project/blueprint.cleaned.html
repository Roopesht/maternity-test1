🤰 Maternal Care Platform

Multi-Tenant PaaS/SaaS for Hospital Antenatal, Labour Room & Delivery Management

Tagline: Customisation · Antenatal · LR · Summing Engine · Billing · In-App Chat · Delivery Archive

📋 System Overview

▼

A multi-tenant platform that lets hospitals manage the full maternity journey — antenatal registration, document uploads, labour room (LR) workflow, delivery/admission, billing, and patient archival — under a single, standardised paid-tier product with minimal manual hand-holding. Each hospital (tenant) configures its own departments, packages, and rate lists; a platform-level Super Admin governs onboarding, cross-tenant audit, complaint escalation, platform monitoring, and usage billing.

🤰 Antenatal-to-Discharge Journey

Covers registration, document uploading, EDD arbitration, labour room gating, delivery/admission, and discharge receipt as the final document.

🧮 Summing Engine

Central charge aggregator across 4 delivery categories and 2 rate lists, with package caps and override handling.

🏢 Multi-Tenant Configuration

Hospital Admin owns all configuration — departments, staff, rate lists, branding. Identical whether the tenant is on PaaS or SaaS.

🗃️ Delivery Archive

Natural vs. Forced archive paths with strict ACTIVE ↔ ARCHIVED status transitions and a defined protocol.

💬 In-App Chat

Scoped strictly to Patient ↔ RMO/Lady Doctor ↔ Hospital Admin, and only while the patient is ACTIVE. Includes an unresolved "N-Chaining" concept open.

🪙 Tenant-Chosen Subscription NEW

Every tenant picks one billing model: Coin-based (50 coins/registration), Double Delivery Figure, or Fixed Subscription slabs.

🔀 PaaS → SaaS Journey

Trademark, branding, own app, own store listing, self-hosting — now framed explicitly as a tracked tenant "journey" with a PaaS/SaaS identification flag.

🌐 Cross-Tenant Patient Traversal NEW

Patients (never tenants) can be looked up/traverse across hospitals — feeds a "global" tier of the Search Service.

Key Stakeholders & Users:
  • Super Admin — Platform-level; ratifies hospital onboarding, runs cross-hospital override audits, tracks usage/API-cost billing, oversees the PaaS→SaaS journey, receives escalated complaints, and owns the platform monitoring/outage dashboard
  • Hospital Admin — Owns all tenant configuration: departments, packages, rate lists, staff, branding (incl. SaaS-side branding); can merge/demerge staff roles; handles manual overrides and complaint intake/forwarding; issues mistake highlights/reprimands
  • Registration Staff (USG Staff / Lady Doctor) — The only roles permitted to register a patient, upload documents, and set EDD
  • Lab Staff NEW — Can add charges and upload documents, but cannot register a patient or set EDD
  • LR Staff — Unlocks Gate 1, flags LSCS/NICU, overrides delivery charges (if present in rate list)
  • Billing Reception — Adds/overrides charges once the patient is past Gate 1
  • RMO, Incident Manager — Clinical support and always-on escalation
  • Patient — Self-service search, chat access while ACTIVE only, complaints auto-routed to Super Admin/Hospital Admin

Registration Flow

  • Onboarding begins when a hospital starts registration; a government certificate/receipt proving the hospital's existence must be uploaded.
  • Upload grants temporary approval immediately; an offline Super Admin later ratifies the hospital or stops the service if the document doesn't validate.
  • The "250k" initialization step (see Subscription & Billing Models) is also part of hospital onboarding.
  • Header and footer branding must be uploaded before the hospital can proceed with example/sample configuration.

Departments

Each hospital decides which departments are present, independently: USG, Lab, NICU, LSCS, RMO, Lady Doctor, LR Staff, Billing Reception. Any combination — including none — is allowed at setup, but once decided, the selection is locked (not changeable later).

🔀 PaaS → SaaS Journey

▼

Configuration is identical whether a hospital is on PaaS or SaaS — Hospital Admin owns all configuration either way. "61. Changes i" reframes this upgrade path as a tracked journey: the platform explicitly identifies/flags whether a tenant is on PaaS or SaaS, and Hospital Admin now owns branding specifically at the "SaaS journey receiver end." SaaS adds exactly four differentiators on top of the PaaS base:

®️ Trademark Registration

Significant independence via the hospital's own registered trademark (assisted, 3-step document process; 2-step follow-up).

📱 Own Signature App

Hospital gets its own branded app rather than operating inside the shared platform app.

🏪 Direct Store Listing

Direct Play Store / Apple Store presence (own developer listing), not bundled under the platform's account.

🖥️ Self Server Hosting

Option to self-host rather than run on shared platform infrastructure.

An automated procedure is planned for Play Store / App Store submission; the hospital supplies its own branded APK for submission. Exact submission tooling is still pending (see Open Decisions).
🌐 World Wide Setup NEW: "61. Changes i" adds a "World Wide Setup" item alongside the PaaS→SaaS journey and Cross-Tenant Patient Traversal, implying eventual multi-region/international deployment. No further detail is given — tracked here and in Vision & Roadmap as a roadmap item, not a committed spec.

📅 Antenatal, Document Upload & EDD

▼

Registration Staff (USG Staff or Lady Doctor) register the patient and upload documents as part of the antenatal journey. EDD (Expected Date of Delivery) itself is calculated by the new EDD Service, which is deliberately narrower than registration: it accepts only USG-report data.

✅ Resolved — who can register: both USG Staff and Lady Doctor are now explicitly grouped as "Registration Staff" with equal rights to register a patient and upload documents (see Roles & Permissions). This replaces the v0.1 contradiction.

EDD Service rules NEW

  • EDD can be set only from a USG report — never from LMP alone.
  • The USG report may be from this hospital or an outside facility — an external report is acceptable as long as it is uploaded.
  • All patient reports should be uploaded to the platform (via the Antenatal Service / Document Service), including outside USG reports used for EDD.
  • EDD is calculated from the first report where the baby has a detectable heartbeat / fetal activity — not simply the first USG report on file.
  • EDD-setting remains USG's exclusive prerogative — Registration Staff enters the registration, but the EDD value itself is derived by the EDD Service from the qualifying report.
Interaction with the v0.1 tolerance/freeze rules not fully specified: "61. Changes i" defines the EDD source (first heartbeat-positive USG report, any facility) but does not explicitly restate whether the v0.1 tolerance windows and freeze rule (below) still apply unchanged on top of this new sourcing rule, or whether they are superseded. Treated here as complementary — sourcing rule determines which report counts; tolerance/freeze rules determine how discrepancies across multiple qualifying reports are handled — pending confirmation.
Gestational AgeAllowed EDD Tolerance
Up to 34 weeks± 2 weeks (e.g., LMP EDD of 28.09.26 must fall between 15.09.26 and 11.10.26)

Freeze rule: if a patient is scanned multiple times across these windows (<34, 34–35, >35 weeks), the earliest EDD value is taken, frozen, and never recalculated afterward.

  • There is no "Gate 0" — that stage is already covered by registration/EDD.
  • G1 and G2 relate to the Natural Archive path; G3 relates to the Forced Archive path.
  • Forced archive triggers one month after EDD expiry with no patient show — the patient can still come in at any point during that one-month window (e.g., on the last day of expiry).
  • If G1 has already been crossed, G3 (forced archive) is disabled.
  • Once past Gate 1, the workflow becomes a simple hospital service: charges are added and the package limit applies (LR Room/NICU staff aren't separately gated here, since there's no inter-service summation or package discount beyond the configured/overridden amount).

🧮 Summing Engine & Charges

▼

The Summing Engine aggregates charges across 4 delivery categories and 2 rate lists, and is the arbiter of the package cap concept (applies only to Normal Delivery). The discharge receipt is the final document once summing is complete — and once it's issued, all other documents on that case become final too.

CategoryAmount TypeNotes
Normal DeliveryProper amountSubject to summing engine; package cap stops standard charges at the declared limit
Abnormal DeliveryToken amount~30% of deliveries; normal package logic doesn't apply, so charge bounce is expected (e.g., ₹12,000 → ₹15,000) for complications like breech, face presentation, oxygen, blood transfusion, vacuum-assist, or extended delivery time
LSCSToken amount~50% of Abnormal Delivery cases escalate to LSCS (≈15% of all deliveries are neither Normal nor LSCS outright — they pass through Abnormal first). Example configuration from source: LSCS adds ₹20,000 on top of the ₹12,000 Normal Delivery (ND) base.
NICUToken amountCan attach on top of Normal, Abnormal, or LSCS — a 4th, independent situation. Example configuration from source: NICU adds ₹25,000 besides the ₹12,000 ND base.

These per-category amounts are LR Staff-configured at the point delivery is confirmed (as soon as LSCS and/or NICU is flagged, the corresponding add-on charge is applied) — this is also why there's no separate "LSCS Room staff" or "NICU staff" role: there's no inter-service summation or package discount at that stage, only the configured amount or an override.

Rate ListAmount TypeNotes
USGProper amountTentative, hospital-configured
LabProper amountTentative, hospital-configured; Lab Staff can add charges here but cannot register/set EDD
OPD—No rate list exists; configuration still pending
Charge flow, no override: normal summation applies as configured.
Charge flow, override: requires an automatic, system-generated explanation for why and what was overridden, reflected across all counters except USG and adders/enterers. Applies across all rate lists/charges, not just delivery. Override is never available before delivery.

🪙 Subscription & Billing Models

▼

New in v0.2: a hospital is no longer assumed to be on the coin ledger by default — each tenant now chooses one of three subscription/billing models. The Subscription Service is being generalised from a single coin ledger into a model-selectable billing engine.

Model 1
🪙 Coin-Based
  • 50 coins deducted per registration entry
  • A "transaction" = any addition of payment
  • 1% price increments permitted above 10,000 coins
Model 2 NEW
📊 Double Delivery Figure
  • Source terms: "Original Delivery Averages" / "Double Delivery Numbers"
  • Reads as: subscription cost tracks roughly double the hospital's average delivery volume/revenue figure
  • Exact formula not specified — see Open Decisions
Model 3 NEW
📐 Fixed Subscription
  • Source term: "Fixed Subscription Slabs"
  • Starts at ₹5,000
  • Escalates up to "triple delivery numbers"
  • Slab boundaries not specified — see Open Decisions

💬 Chat & Notifications

▼

In-App Chat

  • Participants limited strictly to: Patient ↔ RMO/Lady Doctor ↔ Hospital Admin.
  • ANC, USG, and Lab staff cannot chat, except the Lady Doctor.
  • Billing Reception has no in-app chat access.
  • Chat is disabled as soon as LR Staff notifies "Delivery done", or on forced archive — not only on generic archive as v0.1 stated.
  • Resolved: when more than one Lady Doctor is assigned to a case, RMO's chat/clinical duties are carried by the Lady Doctor(s) — see Roles & Permissions.
  • "N-Chaining" — mentioned alongside in-app chat with no elaboration open. Possibly related to the undefined "N-chain" / "N-Loggers" terms from v0.1 — see Open Decisions.

Notification Service — alerts are broader in v0.2

Source framing: "everything is an alert — anything done by anybody or by the system itself becomes an alert." Alert categories called out explicitly:

  • Incident / system alerts
  • Subscription alerts
  • Branding alerts
  • Complaint alerts
  • Overriding alerts
  • Coin-ledger entry alerts (e.g. a 50-coin deduction entry)
  • ...and other events, per the source's open-ended "etc."
  • Weekly and monthly SMS reports are sent to Hospital Admin via the Notification Service.
  • On-call alerting uses AWS SNS directly — no third-party paging tool required.

🔍 Reconciliation & Overrides

▼
Preserve-all-entries policy: wrong entries and overridden entries are never deleted — they are preserved permanently. This full history enables staff appraisals and a reprimand flow that assigns accountability, now explicitly owned by Hospital Admin as "Mistake High Light / Reprimand" (role-restricted — see Roles & Permissions).
  • Manual overrides can also be performed by Billing Reception (equivalent to LR Staff rights) once past Gate 1; the 90-day cooldown no longer applies at that point.
  • Manual override now applies across all rate lists/charges, not only delivery charges.
  • Override audit reports are available to Super Admin across all hospitals.
  • Super Admin can view usage breakdown — API calls weighted by operation cost — per hospital, for platform-level billing/capacity insight.
  • Hospital Admin can merge and demerge staff roles — e.g. temporarily combining two role assignments onto one staff member and splitting them apart again. Not yet detailed beyond the label.

"61. Changes i" introduces a platform-wide complaint-routing model and a Super Admin monitoring/outage responsibility. Every operational role now has an explicit complaint path, and the platform is expected to surface outages to Super Admin before anyone else does.

🏢 Multi-Tenancy & Feature Flags

▼
Feature flags: because this is a multi-tenant application, feature flags are needed to track which app version is "live" and which client (hospital) is running which version — important for staged rollouts and version-specific support.

🌐 Cross-Tenant Patient Traversal NEW

▼

A new platform capability from "61. Changes i": patients — never tenants — can traverse across hospitals on the platform. Multi-tenancy still isolates each hospital's operational data, but a patient's identity/history can be looked up across tenant boundaries when needed for continuity of care.

✅ In scope

Patient records/lookup traversing tenant boundaries (e.g. a patient who registers at a second hospital) — backed by the Patient Registry Service's new "cross-tenant records for patients" responsibility and the Search Service's new global tier.

🚫 Out of scope

Tenants themselves are explicitly not traversable/mergeable — each hospital's configuration, staff, and operational data stay isolated per tenant as before.

Not yet specified: which roles can invoke a cross-tenant / global search, what data is visible across tenants versus redacted, and whether the receiving hospital needs patient consent before viewing another tenant's history.
